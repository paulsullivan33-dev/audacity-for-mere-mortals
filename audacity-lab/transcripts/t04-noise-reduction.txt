t04 — noise reduction, the two steps (measured)
================================================================

Input: noisy.wav (t00) — 1.5 s of white
noise alone, then 3.5 s of an 880 Hz
tone mixed with the same noise.

Baselines (sox stat on the two parts):
  noise only (0-1.5 s)   RMS 0.021443
                         peak 0.060230
  tone + noise (1.5-5 s) RMS 0.088704
                         peak 0.255067

Noise Reduction is the two-step effect:
first it learns the noise from a
noise-only selection, then it applies
that knowledge to the whole track. In
this build the effect takes no script
parameters at all (t02) — the pipe can
only trigger the two steps in order:

>>> Import2: Filename=".../audio/noisy.wav"
>>> SelectTime: Start=0 End=1.5
    RelativeTo=ProjectStart
>>> NoiseReduction:
BatchCommand finished: OK
>>> SelectAll:
>>> NoiseReduction:
BatchCommand finished: OK
>>> Export2: Filename=".../out/nr.wav"
Exported to WAV format: .../out/nr.wav

Measured on the exported file:
  noise only   RMS 0.021443 -> 0.010758
               peak 0.060230 -> 0.030243
  tone + noise RMS 0.088704 -> 0.061805
               peak 0.255067 -> 0.177856

The noise-only part fell by half; the
part carrying the tone fell by less.
That difference is what a working noise
reduction looks like in numbers — and
also its limit: the tone's segment lost
level too, because the effect cannot
fully tell tone from noise.
